Import a Zerodha tradebook

  1. Log in to console.zerodha.com with the account you want to import.
  2. Open Reports, then Tradebook. Pick a segment and date range and download the CSV. Repeat for each segment you trade (Equity, F&O, Currency, Commodity).
  3. Choose the matching account below and import the files. Trades already imported are skipped, so overlapping date ranges are safe.

Files are read in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; your data is saved in this browser's storage only.

Accounts

One entry per Zerodha login, for example yourself, a spouse, or an HUF.

Strategies

Each trade can belong to one strategy. Untagged trades show as Unassigned.

Auto-tagging rules

Imported trades are tagged by the first rule whose pattern matches the symbol. Use * as a wildcard: NIFTY*CE matches Nifty calls, BANKNIFTY* matches all Bank Nifty contracts. A pattern without * matches anywhere in the symbol.

Charges and adjustments

P&L from trades is gross. Record brokerage, STT and other charges here as negative amounts (Console's P&L report shows them per account), so net figures match your statement.

Backup

Data lives in this browser only. Export a backup regularly, and import it to move to another browser or device.
